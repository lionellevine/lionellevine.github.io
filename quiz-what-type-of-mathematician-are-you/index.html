No quadrant is better than another.

Artisanal
Mathematics by and for humans. Each theorem, definition, and proof is bespoke, valued for its beauty and for the understanding it produces.
Industrial
AI as a collaborator and an accelerant. AI systems take part in proof and discovery, and the output often includes a machine-checkable formal proof.
Personal
Motivated by your own curiosity: the desire to know the truth, and to discover or create beauty.
Civic
Motivated by communities beyond mathematics, up to humanity and the planet. In particular, helping humanity steer and adapt to the disruptions caused by our own technologies.

About the axes

The horizontal axis is how much technology is in your mathematical workflow: from mathematics by hand, for and by humans, to AI as a collaborator and accelerant. The vertical axis is how large a community motivates you: from your own curiosity, through collaborators and students, a field, all of science, to humanity and the planet.

The stances come from the survey Math for AI Safety: An invitation for mathematicians, which invites mathematicians to try the civic one and offers open problems to start on.

If you tick the box under your result, your ten answers and your coordinates are recorded, with no name, address, or other identifying information, for a heatmap of where mathematicians land. A quick human check keeps bots off the map.
